Roof Cleaning Questions, Answered

Will soft washing damage my roof?

No. We use low-pressure, manufacturer-recommended soft wash methods designed specifically for roofing materials, not high-pressure blasting.

What happens if it rains on my cleaning day?

If the weather isn’t right, we’ll reschedule you to the next available slot that works for you. There’s no fee to reschedule due to weather.

Do I need to be home?

Not necessarily. As long as we have access to your roof, water, and the areas we’re cleaning, we can complete the job and send your Roof Health Photo Report afterward.

How long does a typical job take?

Most homes are completed in about half a day or less, depending on roof size and condition.

How do you protect my plants and landscaping?

We pre-rinse and cover sensitive areas, manage runoff carefully, and rinse everything again when we’re done to keep your plants safe.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and checks at the time of service.

Do you offer other exterior cleaning services?

Yes. Ask about our house washing, driveway and concrete cleaning, and other exterior services during your quote.
